_Last updated: 6 September 2026_

CmdImpact can earn from clearly labeled sponsorship on educational content. Sponsorship is disabled unless the site operator supplies a sponsor name, message and HTTPS destination at build time.

## No sponsors in product workflows

The terminal workspace at `/app/` and standalone checker at `/check/` do not show sponsorship. Sponsor messages are not placed beside terminal input, output, paste warnings, evidence or action buttons. An authenticated workspace also omits the marketing navigation and footer.

## Guide placement

When enabled, a sponsor message may appear below selected editorial guide content. Every placement is labeled "Sponsored." It is plain text with an external HTTPS link: no sponsor script, tracking pixel, image or cookie is loaded. A placement must not imitate a terminal command, warning, download button or official recommendation.

## Independence

A sponsor cannot pay to remove a finding, change rule severity, receive favorable guide coverage or make a command appear approved. Guard results come only from the published deterministic rules.

CmdImpact does not currently use affiliate links or sponsored articles. Any future commercial relationship will be disclosed beside the relevant content.

## Privacy

CmdImpact does not send data to a sponsor. Following the sponsor link makes a normal browser request to the sponsor's site, which applies its own privacy terms. See the [privacy policy](/privacy/) for the boundary between product and guide pages.
